Privacy policy

Atlas is a private assistant used by a single person: its owner. No other users can sign in.

  • Atlas accesses the owner's own Google account (Gmail and Calendar) only with the owner's consent.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer to summarize and organize the owner's mail and calendar. Text may be sent to the owner's AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to perform this processing on the owner's behalf.
  • Data is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Atlas's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
